OberlinKids

OberlinKids is a nonprofit organization committed to providing support to the general Oberlin community through early childhood screenings, events, monthly e-newsletters, parent groups, and so much more. They support parents and families of children ages 0–5 and provide the resources necessary to support their child’s development. The goal is to give each child from birth to kindergarten who lives in Oberlin the opportunity to be successful in school and in life, and to support our children, families, and community.
