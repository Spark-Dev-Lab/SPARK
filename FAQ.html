FAQ

Before my visit

Who can participate?

We conduct research with children aged 6 months to 5 years, but at any given time we may only be recruiting for specific age ranges within that range.

How can I get my child involved in studies?

You can sign up to be contacted about future research opportunities for you and your child by providing your information via the Participate tab.

How long does a study last? How many times do I need to come in?

Studies typically last only about 15-30 minutes, but visits are typically an hour long to provide you with adequate time to fill out forms and ensure you and your child are comfortable with our team. You only need to come in once.

When are appointments scheduled?

When your child reaches the age eligibility for one of our studies, we will typically call inviting you to participate and ask a few screening questions before we schedule an appointment. Appointments typically occur between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM Monday through Friday, but we regularly schedule study appointments on weekends as well!

How do I get to the lab?

Our lab is located in Severance Hall Room 008, 120 W Lorain St, Oberlin, OH 44074. When you arrive, a Research Assistant will meet you and your child to guide you to our laboratory space.

Are the studies online or in-person?

Most of our studies are conducted in person at our laboratory in Severance Hall on the Oberlin College campus, but we may offer online studies in the future. We strive to make every visit convenient and enjoyable for both you and your child.

How did you get my contact information?

In most cases we got your contact information through you filling out a form indicating your interest in being contacted about research opportunities. If you received a single informational mailout about our research, then we received your name and address from a partner organization such as OberlinKids.

During my visit

Will I be with my child during the study?

Yes, you will remain with your child for the duration of your study visit, although sometimes parents may choose to briefly leave their child with a research team member to accept a call or use the restroom if they wish.

Can I bring my child's siblings with me to an in-person study?

You are welcome to bring your child's siblings with you, but please provide us with at least 24 to 48 hours notice if you intend to do so. One caregiver needs to be in the room with the child participating in the study; however, if you notify us ahead of time, a babysitter (research assistant) will be present and can keep your non-participating child company.

After my visit

Does my child receive anything for participating?

Yes. Depending on your child's age, you will be given an age-appropriate gift for your child as a thank you for participating.

Will I receive a copy of the results and/or publication when the study is completed?

Although we do not share results from individual participants or send publications when the study is completed, we provide updates in our bi-monthly newsletter, which includes information about our research, team members, and insights from scientific research on child development. We also post updates on our website and social media pages.
